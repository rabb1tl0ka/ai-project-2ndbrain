# Statement of Work — Customer Service Bot POC
**Client**: ACME Corp
**Vendor**: Loka Inc.
**SOW Number**: SOW-002
**Effective Date**: January 15, 2026
**End Date**: March 31, 2026

---

## Engagement Scope

Following the AI Readiness Assessment (SOW-001), Loka will design and build a proof-of-concept customer service chatbot for ACME Corp. The POC will demonstrate automated handling of ACME's top 10 customer inquiry categories using a RAG-based architecture grounded on ACME's existing knowledge base.

## Deliverables

1. **Technical Architecture Document** — System design, data flow, and infrastructure requirements. Due: January 31, 2026.
2. **Working POC** — Functional chatbot handling top 10 inquiry categories with >80% accuracy on test set. Due: March 15, 2026.
3. **Evaluation Report** — Performance metrics, limitations, and go/no-go recommendation for production. Due: March 31, 2026.
