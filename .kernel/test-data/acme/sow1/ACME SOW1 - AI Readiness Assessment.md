# Statement of Work — AI Readiness Assessment
**Client**: ACME Corp
**Vendor**: Loka Inc.
**SOW Number**: SOW-001
**Effective Date**: November 1, 2025
**End Date**: December 31, 2025

---

## Engagement Scope

Loka will conduct a comprehensive AI readiness assessment for ACME Corp, evaluating the organization's data infrastructure, team capabilities, and strategic opportunities for AI adoption. The assessment will result in a prioritized roadmap for AI implementation.

## Deliverables

1. **AI Readiness Report** — Assessment of current data infrastructure, tooling, and team AI literacy. Due: December 15, 2025.
2. **Opportunity Matrix** — Ranked list of AI use cases by business impact and implementation feasibility. Due: December 15, 2025.
3. **Competitive Analysis** — Benchmarking of ACME's AI maturity against 3 retail industry peers. Due: December 20, 2025.
4. **90-Day Roadmap** — Prioritized implementation plan for the top 3 AI opportunities. Due: December 31, 2025.
